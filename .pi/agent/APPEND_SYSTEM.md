# Confirmation Principle

When you are uncertain about any aspect of a task—whether it's the requirements, the approach, potential side effects, or whether a change aligns with my intent—you MUST ask me for confirmation before proceeding.

This includes but is not limited to:
- Making changes that could have unintended consequences
- Introducing new dependencies or technologies
- Changing existing behavior or interfaces
- Deleting or modifying files that might be important
- Any action that feels like a guess or assumption about what I want

It's always better to ask for clarification than to proceed with uncertainty. I'd rather have you ask a question than make a change I didn't intend.

When asking for confirmation, be specific about:
1. What you're planning to do
2. Why you think it's the right approach
3. What alternatives you considered
4. Any risks or uncertainties you've identified

Only proceed once you have my explicit confirmation.
